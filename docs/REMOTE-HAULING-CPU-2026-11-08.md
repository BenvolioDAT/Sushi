## Changes and protections

- Productive remote Freighters can use a narrow delivery allowance, bounded by projected total CPU <=90% of baseline and bucket >=6000. Fresh completed-tick measurements, reload warmup and a noncritical CPU mode are required. The home must have healthy source WORK, local CARRY, a lasting Foreman and no known threat. Physically stationed remote miners, safe known routes and fresh unreserved energy justify the allowance. The final body is capped to the recurring production deficit; backlog cannot enlarge it. Another queued/spawning remote hauler prevents duplicate relief.
- One previously productive remote mine can restart when all remote mining has stopped, provided existing healthy remote haulers cover its entire output. Mining/delivery records within 1500 ticks and safe ownership intel within 500 ticks are required; without timed ownership intel, a fresh 25-tick observation is required. Stale or hostile ownership blocks it. Restarting production does not require a fresh stockpile scan, since the miner creates new energy. This uses paid-for hauling capacity rather than starting an unsupported fleet. Fresh friendly reservations update production demand immediately, before portfolio rescoring.
- Pending CPU includes spawning bodies, accepted intents not yet visible, and the first 30 active ticks of newborns, deduplicated by name. The extra candidate reserves at least 0.5 CPU. Energy reserves, spawn load, role limits and the hard population cap remain enforced. Ordinary expansion retains its original CPU ceiling. The delivery/restart allowance can be disabled or tightened through capacity settings, but cannot exceed 90%.
- Active Artificers are classified by current task/category; incoming critical workers keep their protection. Role CPU samples older than 500 ticks, or in the future after rollback, revert to the existing fallback plus traffic overhead. Fresh and conservative untimed legacy samples remain honored.
- A single full-source aging local miner uses its named lifetime handoff even with spare seats, aligning planning and admission and preventing redundant growth requests.
- Live return-trip inspection found that stockpile overflow erased remote source provenance immediately after a successful `drop` intent, before the engine changed Store. Cargo reached home, but `lastDeliveryAt` stayed stale and remote income remained discounted. Remote overflow now retains provenance until the following tick's existing cargo-delta reconciliation confirms delivery, before empty-worker cleanup. A deferred-Store regression failed before this correction and passed afterward.
